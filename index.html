TRY THE WORK BEFORE CHOOSING

Four mini career experiences.

Try a small, safe exercise. Then rate how interesting and comfortable it felt. This is reflection, not a professional assessment.

01 · TECHNOLOGY

Think like a problem-solver

A school club has 24 members and wants equal teams of 4. What simple steps would you use to find the number of teams and explain your answer?

02 · BUSINESS & MARKETING

Understand an audience

A local stationery shop wants more student customers. Name one student group, one need they have, and one useful message the shop could share.

03 · CREATIVE COMMUNICATION

Create a clear message

A school event needs a poster. Choose a headline, three essential details, and a color or visual idea that makes the information easy to understand.

04 · PEOPLE & COMMUNITY

Plan a helpful activity

A student group wants to help new classmates settle in. Suggest one welcoming activity, who should help organize it, and how you would know it worked.

Your written challenge responses stay in this browser session and are not sent to RaahLab. Do not enter personal or sensitive information.
